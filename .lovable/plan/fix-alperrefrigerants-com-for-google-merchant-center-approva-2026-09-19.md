```json
"hasCertification": {
  "@type": "Certification",
  "certificationAuthority": "AHRI",
  "certificationCode": "AHRI-CERTIFIED",
  "name": "AHRI Certified"
```

Placement note: schema.org's `hasCertification` is a property of Product, not of Offer. The audit asks for it "inside product offers structured data"; I will attach it to the Product node (the valid position that Google's Rich Results Test accepts) since nesting it inside the Offer would be invalid markup.
