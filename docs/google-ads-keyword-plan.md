## Structure rules

- One ad group per gas code (R-410A, R-22, R-454B, R-134a, R-32, R-513A, R-438A, R-422B, R-421A, R-452A, R-404A, R-407C, R-1234yf, R-290, R-744, R-507A).
- Tier 1 gas codes: phrase + exact. Tier 3: exact only.
- Wholesale/distributor/pricing terms consolidate into one "Wholesale & Bulk" ad group pointed at `/bulk-pricing`.
- AC terms point at `/products/air-conditioners` and must NOT carry EPA 608 / DOT HazMat language.
- Refrigerant ad copy must carry EPA 608 certified-buyer / B2B wholesale language.
